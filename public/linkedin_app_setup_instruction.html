How to Connect Your LinkedIn Account to Our App

A simple, non-technical guide for creating a LinkedIn developer app, finding the Client ID and Client Secret, and entering them into our application.

Please do not share your Client Secret publicly. Only enter it inside the secure LinkedIn settings page of our app.

What you need before starting

Your personal LinkedIn account
A LinkedIn Company Page
Permission to manage that Page
Our app's Redirect URL
A square app logo
About 10–15 minutes
The LinkedIn Company Page is required only to register the developer app. You can still connect and publish to your personal LinkedIn profile.
1

Sign in to LinkedIn Developers

Use the same LinkedIn account that will own the developer app.
  1. Open your web browser.
  2. Visit LinkedIn Developers.
  3. Click Sign in.
  4. Enter your normal LinkedIn email and password.
  5. After signing in, click My apps in the top menu.
2

Create a LinkedIn Company Page, if you do not have one

LinkedIn does not allow a personal profile in the “LinkedIn Page” field.
  1. On LinkedIn, click the For Business menu.
  2. Choose Create a Company Page.
  3. Select the most suitable Page type, usually Company.
  4. Enter a Page name, such as Your Business Name or Your App Name.
  5. Add a public LinkedIn Page URL.
  6. Add a website URL if LinkedIn asks for one.
  7. Select an industry, company size, and company type.
  8. Upload a logo.
  9. Confirm that you are authorized to represent the organization.
  10. Click Create Page.
You must be an administrator of the LinkedIn Page. A normal personal-profile URL will not work.
3

Create the LinkedIn developer app

This creates the credentials that our app needs.
  1. Return to LinkedIn Developers.
  2. Click My apps.
  3. Click Create app.
  4. Complete the form using the table below.
LinkedIn field What the user should enter
App name Enter a clear name, for example My Social Post Scheduler.
LinkedIn Page Search for and select the Company Page created in Step 2.
Privacy policy URL Enter the privacy-policy URL supplied by our app or your business.
App logo Upload a clear square logo. LinkedIn recommends at least one dimension of 100 pixels.
Legal agreement Read the agreement and select the checkbox.
  1. Click Create app.
  2. If LinkedIn asks for Page verification, follow the on-screen instructions.
4

Add the required LinkedIn products

These products allow login and posting to the personal feed.
  1. Open the newly created app.
  2. Click the Products tab.
  3. Find Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect.
  4. Click Request access or Add product.
  5. Find Share on LinkedIn.
  6. Click Request access or Add product.
  7. Wait until the products show as available or approved.
The Share on LinkedIn product provides the w_member_social permission used to post on behalf of the connected member.
5

Add our Redirect URL

This tells LinkedIn where to return the user after permission is approved.
  1. Inside your LinkedIn developer app, click the Auth tab.
  2. Scroll to OAuth 2.0 settings.
  3. Find Authorized redirect URLs for your app.
  4. Click the pencil/edit icon.
  5. Copy the Redirect URL shown inside our app's LinkedIn setup page.
  6. Paste it into LinkedIn exactly as provided.
  7. Click Update or Save.

6

Copy the Client ID

The Client ID identifies the LinkedIn developer app.
  1. Stay on the Auth tab.
  2. Find the section named Application credentials or Authentication keys.
  3. Find Client ID.
  4. Click the copy icon, or carefully select and copy the value.
  5. Return to our app.
  6. Open Settings → LinkedIn Integration.
  7. Paste it into the LinkedIn Client ID field.
Example only:
78abc123xyz456
7

Reveal and copy the Client Secret

This is the secret code that proves the app is genuine.
  1. On the same Auth page, find Primary Client Secret or Client Secret.
  2. Click Show or the eye icon.
  3. LinkedIn may ask you to enter your LinkedIn password again.
  4. Copy the complete Client Secret.
  5. Return to our app.
  6. Paste it into the LinkedIn Client Secret field.
  7. Click Save LinkedIn Settings.
Never send the Client Secret by email, WhatsApp, screenshot, or public chat. Do not put it in a public GitHub repository. Treat it like a password.
8

Connect the personal LinkedIn profile

Creating the developer app does not connect the personal profile automatically.
  1. In our app, open Settings → Social Accounts.
  2. Click Connect LinkedIn.
  3. You will be sent to LinkedIn.
  4. Sign in if LinkedIn asks you to sign in.
  5. Review the requested permissions.
  6. Click Allow.
  7. LinkedIn will return you to our app.
  8. Confirm that the LinkedIn account shows as Connected.
The user should never type their LinkedIn password inside our app. The password is entered only on LinkedIn's own website.
9

Create and schedule the first LinkedIn post

Use a small test post before scheduling important content.
  1. Open the post composer in our app.
  2. Select the connected LinkedIn account.
  3. Write a short test message.
  4. Select Post now for the first test.
  5. Confirm that the post appears on the personal LinkedIn profile.
  6. Create another post and choose Schedule.
  7. Select the correct date, time, and timezone.
  8. Click Schedule post.

Setup is complete when the LinkedIn profile is shown as connected and a test post can be published successfully.
