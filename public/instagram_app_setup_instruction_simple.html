Instagram Connection Setup Guide

A simple step-by-step guide for connecting an Instagram account so posts can be created and scheduled from the application.

1. Before You Start

Before creating the Instagram app, make sure these items are ready:

  • A Meta/Facebook account.
  • An Instagram account that you want to connect.
  • The Instagram account should be a Professional account, such as Business or Creator.
  • Access to your application’s Social Integrations page.
  • A secure website link for your application. The link should start with https://.
Simple meaning: Meta controls Instagram connection. You need to create an app in Meta Developer first, then connect that app with your own system.

2. Create the Instagram App

1

Open Meta for Developers

Go to Meta for Developers and log in with your Facebook/Meta account.

2

Create a New App

Click My Apps, then click Create App.

3

Enter App Information

Enter your app name and contact email. Then continue to the next step.

3. Select the Correct Use Case

When Meta asks what you want to do with the app, select:

Content management → Manage messaging & content on Instagram

This option is suitable for connecting Instagram and managing Instagram posts from your application.

4. Add Required Permissions

After the app is created, open the Instagram setup page from your app dashboard.

1

Open Instagram Setup

Go to Use cases, then click Customize beside the Instagram use case.

2

Add Permissions

Find Add required permissions and click Add all required permissions.

Note: If you want to publish or schedule Instagram posts, make sure Instagram content publishing permission is included. If you are not sure, ask your technical support team to verify the permission list.

5. Add Instagram Tester

Before the app is publicly approved by Meta, you can test it only with approved tester accounts.

1

Add Tester From Meta Dashboard

Go to App roles → Roles. Add the Instagram username as an Instagram Tester.

2

Accept the Tester Invitation

Log in to that Instagram account. Then go to:

Instagram → Settings and privacy → Website permissions → Tester invites

Accept the invitation.

3

Check Status

Return to Meta Developer dashboard and refresh the page. The tester status should no longer show as Pending.

6. Set Up Instagram Business Login

From the Instagram setup page, find Set up Instagram business login and click Set up.

1

Add Website Redirect Link

Add the secure callback link provided by your application/team.

Example format:

https://your-website.com/social-integrations/instagram/callback

2

Save the Setup

Click Save. If Meta shows an error, make sure the link starts with https://.

Important: Do not use a normal localhost link here. Meta usually requires a secure public link for Instagram login.

7. Connect Instagram in the Application

After the Meta setup is complete, go to your own application.

  1. Open the Social Integrations page.
  2. Find the Instagram section.
  3. Click Connect Instagram.
  4. Log in with the Instagram account.
  5. Allow the requested permissions.
  6. After successful connection, the application should show the Instagram account as Connected.
Connected means ready: After this, the Instagram account can be selected while creating or scheduling posts.

8. Test Posting and Scheduling

Once Instagram is connected, test the full process carefully.

1

Create a Test Post

Go to the Add Post page. Create a simple post with an image or video.

2

Select Instagram Account

Choose Instagram as the platform and select the connected Instagram account.

3

Schedule the Post

Set the posting date and time, then save the schedule.

4

Confirm Result

Check the schedule list and confirm that the post shows the correct Instagram platform, account, date, time, and status.

9. Common Problems and Solutions

Problem What It Means What To Do
Tester status shows Pending The Instagram user has not accepted the invite. Open Instagram settings and accept the tester invitation.
Insufficient developer role The Instagram account is not approved as a tester yet. Add the Instagram account as a tester and accept the invitation.
Redirect URL error The callback link is not accepted by Meta. Use a secure link that starts with https://.
Instagram account does not connect The account may not be a professional account or permission was denied. Change the Instagram account to Business/Creator and try again.
Post does not publish Permission, media, or account connection may be invalid. Reconnect Instagram and try with a simple image post first.

Final Checklist

  • Meta app created successfully.
  • Instagram use case selected.
  • Required permissions added.
  • Instagram tester added and accepted.
  • Instagram Business Login setup completed.
  • Instagram connected from the Social Integrations page.
  • Test post created and scheduled successfully.

Keep this guide for future reference when connecting another Instagram account or helping another user complete the setup.
